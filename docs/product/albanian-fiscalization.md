# Albanian Fiscalization Product Contract

Status: implementation foundation, not yet certified for production fiscalization.

## Source hierarchy

Use the Albanian General Directorate of Taxation (DPT) technical specifications and legislation as the source of truth. Competitor behavior is useful for UX/product discovery, but must never override the official schema or validation rules.

Official technical documents:
- https://www.tatime.gov.al/c/424/614/622/fiskalizimi-specifikime-teknike
- https://www.tatime.gov.al/c/424/444/pyetje-pergjigje-mbi-fiskalizimin
- https://www.tatime.gov.al/c/424/494/lista-e-subjekteve-te-certifikuara

## Fiscal identifiers

- NSLF / IIC: issuer-side invoice security identifier. It is generated by the fiscalization software from the prescribed invoice identity fields, digitally signed with the taxpayer certificate/private key, then derived from the signature according to the DPT algorithm.
- NIVF / FIC: unique fiscal invoice identifier returned by the Central Information System after successful validation/fiscalization.
- QR: must resolve to the official verification payload/link returned or constructed according to the DPT specification. Never invent a QR verification result.
- Offline invoice: may have NSLF/IIC before it receives NIVF/FIC; it must enter the retry/subsequent-delivery workflow.

## Cash versus non-cash

DPT payment method families:
- CASH: BANKNOTE, CARD, CHECK, SVOUCHER, COMPANY, ORDER.
- NONCASH: ACCOUNT, FACTORING, COMPENSATION, TRANSFER, WAIVER, KIND, OTHER.

Current internal mapping:
- cash -> BANKNOTE / CASH
- card -> CARD / CASH
- bank_transfer -> ACCOUNT / NONCASH
- other -> OTHER / NONCASH

A single fiscal invoice must not mix payment methods that belong to incompatible CASH and NONCASH invoice families. Internal mixed payments remain supported operationally; fiscalization readiness must require a legally compatible document split when both families occur.

## Hospitality invoice presentation

A4 and receipt layouts should contain:
- seller legal name, address and NUIS/NIPT
- issue date/time
- fiscal invoice number
- operator code
- business unit code
- TCR/device code when applicable
- CASH/NONCASH invoice type
- immutable item snapshots: description, unit, quantity, unit price before VAT, discount, VAT rate, net, VAT, gross
- totals and VAT distribution
- NSLF/IIC and NIVF/FIC
- payment method breakdown
- verification QR

Cash label in Albanian UI: `Kartëmonedha dhe monedha`.

## Security contract

- Never store raw certificate passwords or private keys in business tables.
- `certificate_secret_ref` is a secret reference only.
- Production keys must come from a secret manager/KMS/Vault or another audited encrypted secret provider.
- XML/SOAP payloads and provider errors may be audited, but private keys, certificate passwords, tokens and sensitive buyer data must not be written to application logs.
- Fiscalization attempts are append-only operational records.

## Correction contract

- Issued invoices are immutable.
- Corrections create linked credit/corrective documents; they do not edit/delete the original invoice.
- Fiscal corrective invoices must reference the original fiscal identity as required by DPT (including original NSLF/IIC and issue identity).
- Payment refunds after invoice issuance require an authorized correction chain.

## Certification

The software cannot be marketed as a certified Albanian fiscalization solution until the producer/maintainer and solution have completed the applicable DPT/AKSHI certification process.

## Production readiness and activation

Production is fail-closed and requires an explicit activation step. Changing certificate identity, software code, VAT-registration facts, or deployment endpoint invalidates prior readiness state as appropriate.

Preflight validates, without exposing secret material:
- business NUIS/NIPT and certified software code
- HTTPS endpoint and deployment-approved endpoint allowlist
- PKCS#12 secret resolution
- X509 not-before / not-after validity
- certificate/private-key match
- certificate SHA-256 fingerprint for operator comparison
- active business-unit codes, TCR codes and operator codes
- successful prior TEST verification before PRODUCTION
- readable DPT/AKSHI CA trust bundle in PRODUCTION
- asynchronous queue driver in PRODUCTION

PRODUCTION activation is owner-only through the dedicated permission and is audited with activation timestamp and actor. Fiscal dispatch checks activation and fresh preflight state again before queueing, while the DPT gateway independently re-checks the approved endpoint and certificate validity before any network call.

Recommended deployment variables:
- `FISCAL_DPT_TEST_ENDPOINT`
- `FISCAL_DPT_PRODUCTION_ENDPOINT`
- `FISCAL_DPT_CA_BUNDLE`
- `FISCAL_SECRET_DIR`
- certificate/password references through `env:`, `secret:`, or an implemented `vault:` provider

## Monitoring

The platform exposes tenant-safe fiscal health metrics:
- invoice and corrective-document states
- attempts and success rate over the last 24 hours
- retry backlog and oldest scheduled retry
- recent safe error diagnostics
- per-document attempt history

Payload hashes, private keys, certificate/password secret references, raw XML credentials and certificate material are never returned by monitoring endpoints.
